Proyekto has four plans, and each one has a job. The plan belongs to the
workspace, not to you, so the same account can be on Free in one workspace and
Business in another. This page describes what each tier *includes*; the
numbers behind each tier, and what it costs, live on the Proyekto pricing page
on the web.

## The four tiers

| Plan | Its job |
| --- | --- |
| **Free** | Plan the work. A real roadmap, real tasks, real chat — enough to run something small end to end. |
| **Pro** | Run real delivery. The governance registers, time tracking, and the higher AI allowance that daily use needs. |
| **Business** | Govern delivery across an organisation. No caps on projects or teams, stronger AI, private teams and a long activity history. |
| **Enterprise** | Identity, audit and account management. SSO and provisioning, granular admin control, exportable history. |

## On every plan, including Free

None of the following is ever gated. They are what Proyekto is:

- The **roadmap canvas**, and epics, features and tasks —
  [how roadmaps work](/docs/roadmaps-and-work/roadmaps-overview)
- All four views of the same data: the roadmap tree, the epic view, the
  timeline with milestones, and the board
- **Roadmap templates** — start from a published template rather than a blank
  canvas
- **Comments and @-mentions** on epics, features and tasks
- **Project chat** — channels, direct messages, attachments, search
- **Meetings**, with recurring series and video links
- **Roadmap share links**, granting viewing or commenting to people with no
  account
- The **AI assistant**, with a monthly message allowance
- **Google sign-in**, and the mobile apps

## What arrives at Pro

Pro is where a project stops being a plan and starts being a delivery
engagement.

- The **delivery registers**: [deliverables](/docs/delivery-governance/deliverables)
  with submit-and-review, [change requests](/docs/delivery-governance/change-requests),
  [risks and issues](/docs/delivery-governance/risks-and-issues), and
  [decisions](/docs/delivery-governance/decisions)
- **Time tracking and timesheets**, with rate cards and payouts —
  [time tracking](/docs/teams-time-and-rates/time-tracking)
- The **MCP server**, which connects Claude and other MCP hosts to your
  projects. It is an optional integration and may not be enabled on your
  workspace — see [Connect Proyekto to Claude](/docs/account-and-apps/mcp-server)
- A **longer activity window** on each project's
  [activity log](/docs/delivery-governance/activity)
- A **higher monthly AI allowance**, and no cap on the number of nodes a single
  roadmap can hold

## What arrives at Business

- **No cap on projects or teams**
- A **higher AI allowance again**, and the assistant runs at **High reasoning
  effort** rather than Standard — it thinks longer before it proposes a change
- **Private teams and guests**: teams hidden from the rest of the workspace,
  and people brought in for one piece of work
- **Full activity history**, rather than a window

## What arrives at Enterprise

- **SAML single sign-on and SCIM provisioning**, so your identity provider
  creates and deactivates members —
  [members and seats](/docs/workspaces-and-plans/members-and-seats)
- **Granular roles and permissions** across the workspace
- **Activity export**, for audit outside Proyekto
- **Priority AI capacity**, on a negotiated allowance
- **Account management** — a named contact rather than a support queue

## Complimentary plans

A workspace can be put on a paid tier by Proyekto rather than by buying it —
for a partner, a pilot, or a community project. A complimentary plan behaves
exactly like that tier: the same features, the same limits, no difference in
behaviour anywhere in the product. You will see a **Complimentary** badge on
the plan, and on the Usage page, together with an end date if it has one.

Because nothing is being purchased, a complimentary workspace is never shown
an upgrade prompt.

## Finding the plan you are on

Open your workspace's **Usage** tab. The Plan section names the tier, shows
the Complimentary badge if there is one, and summarises what it includes; the
sections underneath show what is counted and which features are on. The
**Billing** tab shows the plan alongside seats and invoices.

Both tabs are web pages. The mobile app deliberately carries no surface that
prices or sells anything, so plan changes happen on the web and the app says
so rather than showing a dead button —
[the mobile app](/docs/account-and-apps/mobile-app) explains what else is left
out.

Only a workspace owner can change the plan. Everyone else who runs into a
gated feature is told to ask one.
