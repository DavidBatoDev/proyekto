If someone posts something abusive, hateful, sexual, violent or spammy, you can report it to us and block the person. Both work in the mobile app on Android and iOS and on the web. We have zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users.

## Report a message

- **On a phone:** press and hold the message, then tap **Report message**.
- **On a computer:** hover over the message, click **⋯**, then **Report message**.

Pick the reason that fits best, add a note if it helps, and tap **Submit report**. You can also turn on **Also block** to block the person in the same step.

The person is not told that you reported them.

## Report a comment

Under someone else's comment on an epic, feature or task, click **Report**, then pick a reason.

## Report or block a person

You can report or block someone from three places:

- **A direct message:** tap **⋯** in the conversation header, or open the side panel.
- **Their profile:** tap **⋯** next to their name.
- **Any of their messages**, as described above.

## What blocking does

When you block someone:

- neither of you can send the other a direct message — your conversation stays in your list, with an **Unblock** option where the message box was;
- their messages and comments collapse to a small **Blocked message** placeholder — you can still tap **Show** if you need the context;
- they can no longer notify you: no push, email or in-app notification about them reaches you;
- they are not told that you blocked them.

You still share any projects you were both in, so their work stays visible to everyone else in those projects. If you need them removed from a project, ask a project owner or admin.

## Unblock someone

Open **Settings → Blocked people** and tap **Unblock** next to their name. You can also unblock from their profile or your direct message with them.

## What happens after you report

Every report goes straight to our team, and we review it within 24 hours. If the content breaks our [Terms of Service](/terms), we remove it and may suspend or close the account responsible.

If someone is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services right away.

You can also email **support@proyekto.tech** about anything that worries you.
