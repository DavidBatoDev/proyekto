Selling on Proyekto means putting yourself in front of the people posting work:
as a consultant who leads delivery, or as talent who executes it. All of it is on
the web — the installed Proyekto app leaves the marketplace and the seller pages
out, so set yourself up in a browser and use the app for the delivery work that
follows.

## Consultant or talent

They are capabilities, not account types, and one account can hold both. Neither
is a setting on your profile in the ordinary sense:

- **Consultant** means a verified application. A reviewer at Proyekto approves it.
- **Talent** means an active talent profile. You switch it on yourself.

## Becoming a consultant

Apply from the marketplace's consultant pages. The application asks for who you
are and what you do, your specialities and the years of experience behind each,
links to your work, your LinkedIn profile, the rate you work at, and an identity
document.

Then you wait. Your profile shows that the application is **in review** while a
reviewer looks at it. Three things can happen:

1. **Approved** — your profile becomes **verified** and the unlocks below apply
   straight away.
2. **Sent back** — you revise the application in place and submit it again.
3. **Nothing yet** — review is done by a person, so it is not instant.

Nothing you have in Proyekto depends on the outcome. Your projects, roadmaps and
workspace carry on exactly as before while you wait.

### What verified unlocks

| Unlock | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Talent discovery** | You can browse talent profiles and send hiring invites |
| **Finance surfaces** | Contracts and invoices become available to you — see [Contracts and invoices](/docs/clients-and-marketplace/contracts-and-invoices) |
| **Template publishing** | You can publish roadmap templates for others to start from |
| **Public directory** | Your profile is listed and findable in the category tree |

> Verified is a statement about your application, not a ranking. It is what makes
> the rest of the seller surface appear.

## Becoming talent

Talent has no review step. You build a talent profile — who you are, what you do,
links to your work, your rate — and activating it makes you **discoverable**: you
appear in the talent browse pages and in the category tree, and verified
consultants can find you.

### Go-live: pausing without deleting

Discoverability is a switch, not a state you have to earn again. The **go-live**
control turns it off when you are full and back on when you are not.

Pausing hides you from discovery. It does not delete your profile, your work
links, or anything else you have written — it all stays exactly where it is,
waiting for you to resume. Nothing in your projects is affected either; pausing
only changes whether strangers can find you.

## Hiring invites

A verified consultant who needs a hand browses talent, opens a profile, and sends
a **hiring invite** from it. The invite is an approach, not an assignment.

The recipient sees the invite in Proyekto — who sent it and what they are
inviting them to — and decides. Nothing changes on either side until they accept,
and declining costs the recipient nothing.

An invite is not project access. Even after someone accepts, they see a project
only when a project access record exists for them, which is a separate, deliberate
grant. See [Project access and roles](/docs/projects/access-and-roles).

## Authoring a service

A **service** is an offering you write yourself: a defined piece of work you are
ready to deliver, as opposed to a brief, which a client writes to describe work
that does not exist yet.

Create one from your services page, give it a title and a description of what the
buyer gets, and file it under the category, subcategory and topic where buyers
will look for it. Everything is editable afterwards — a service is a living
listing, not a one-shot post. An untitled one stays yours until you are ready.

Published services appear on your profile and in the category tree, alongside the
consultants and talent in the same topic. See [The
marketplace](/docs/clients-and-marketplace/marketplace) for how buyers navigate
that tree.

## Engagements

An **engagement** is the record that connects a marketplace agreement to the
project work that delivers it. It exists so that an arrangement made in the
marketplace and the delivery work in a project are visibly the same piece of work
rather than two unrelated things.
